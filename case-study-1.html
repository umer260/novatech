FinPulse Dashboard

A real-time analytics platform that helps a fintech lender approve loans faster.

The challenge

FinPulse analysts moved data between five spreadsheets and two legacy tools. A single loan review took two days and errors reached customers.

Our approach

We interviewed twelve analysts, mapped their review steps and built one dashboard that pulls every data source into a single view. Releases went out weekly so analysts could test and correct each step.

Loan decisions now take under one day, and review errors dropped by 74% in the first quarter.
