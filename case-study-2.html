ShopSense Recommender

A recommendation engine that lifted average basket size across a retail marketplace.

AI
+18%Basket size
+11%Repeat purchases
140 msMedian response time
6 weeksTo first A/B test

The challenge

Shoppers saw the same bestsellers everywhere. ShopSense had five years of order data and no way to use it on the product page.

Our approach

We trained a ranking model on purchase history, served it through a low-latency API and ran A/B tests against the old carousel. Product managers could inspect why each item was recommended.

The winning model went live for all users after a four-week test with a 18% lift in basket size.
